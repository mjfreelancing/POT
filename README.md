## About POT

POT is a forward-looking financial management tool designed to answer one critical question: **"Will I have enough money to pay my bills?"**

Unlike traditional budgeting apps that focus on tracking historical spending and categorizing every transaction, POT is built for **projection and planning**. It helps you visualize your future financial position based on:

- Current bank account balances
- Scheduled income (when money comes in)
- Planned expenses (when bills are due)

### How POT is Different

**POT is not a budgeting app.** Here's what makes it unique:

- ✅ **Future-focused** - Projects account balances weeks and months ahead, not just tracking what you've already spent
- ✅ **Bill-centric** - Designed to ensure you never miss a payment due to insufficient funds
- ✅ **What-if scenarios** - Adjust income/expenses or temporarily disable items to see the impact on future cash flow
- ✅ **No bank integration required** - Manual account balance entry keeps the app free and private (no costly API fees or security concerns)
- ✅ **Simple data model** - Income and expenses are linked to specific accounts with frequencies (days, weeks, months, years, or one-time)
- ✅ **End dates or forever** - Model temporary income/expenses (e.g., a 12-month contract) or ongoing commitments

### Why POT Exists

POT was created to solve a real problem: **seeing money in your bank account doesn't tell you if you can actually spend it.**

You might have $5,000 in your account today, but if you have $6,000 in bills due over the next two weeks, you're headed for trouble. Traditional banking apps show your current balance but provide no visibility into upcoming commitments. POT fills that gap by projecting your balance forward based on scheduled income and expenses, so you can see potential shortfalls **before** they happen.

### What POT Does Best

- **Prevents overdrafts** - See in advance if you'll have insufficient funds
- **Plans for large expenses** - Model the impact of major purchases before committing
- **Manages irregular income** - Ideal for freelancers, contractors, or anyone with variable pay schedules
- **Simulates scenarios** - Test "what if I delay this expense?" or "what if I get paid early?"
- **Stays simple** - No transaction imports, no category hierarchies, no complexity — just the essentials

### What POT Doesn't Do

- ❌ **No historical tracking** - Not designed for analyzing past spending patterns
- ❌ **No transaction categorization** - Won't break down your spending by groceries, entertainment, etc.
- ❌ **No bank integration** - You manually update account balances (keeps the app free and private)
- ❌ **No receipt scanning** - Not built for tracking individual purchases
- ❌ **No investment tracking** - Focuses on cash flow, not portfolio management

**In short:** POT is laser-focused on one thing — **making sure you have enough money when your bills are due.**

### Key Features

- 📊 **Financial Projections** - Visualize your account balances months in advance
- 💰 **Expense Tracking** - Manage recurring and one-time expenses
- 💵 **Income Management** - Track multiple income sources and payment schedules
- 🏦 **Account Management** - Monitor balances across all your financial accounts
- 📁 **Data Import/Export** - Backup and restore your financial data
